🌿 Welcome to the Collaboratorium!

We're so glad you're here.

Over the next few weeks, you'll join a small interdisciplinary team to imagine, design, and test a live interactive experience inspired by the stories and values of Fuji Sanctuary.

This isn't a course or a competition.
It's an experiment.

Together we'll explore new ways of creating meaningful experiences that blend story, ritual, technology, and human connection.

You don't need to understand everything before you begin.
We'll guide you one step at a time.

You don't need to be an artist, designer, engineer, storyteller, or technology expert to participate. Every team brings together different strengths and perspectives.

Curiosity, generosity, and a willingness to experiment are far more important than having the "right" skills.

Everything below is the same Participant Guide — just broken into pieces so it's easy to read. Tap a section to open it.
One Final Thought

The Collaboratorium is an invitation to play.

Some things will work beautifully.
Some won't.
Both are valuable.

The purpose isn't to create a perfect experience.
The purpose is to discover something together that none of us could have created alone.

There are no perfect outcomes here. Success means showing up, exploring together, and sharing what you discover.

Welcome to the journey. We can't wait to see what emerges. 🌿
